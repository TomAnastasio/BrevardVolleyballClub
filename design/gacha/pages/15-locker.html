The locker room

What opens when you tap "Change outfit" on your profile. Three layouts below, all working: tap tabs, items and monsters to try them. The items are stand-ins from the wardrobe batch (page 13) until the cuts are in.

Works the same in all three

  • Tap an item to wear it. It's saved straight away, so there's no Save button to forget.
  • "Plain" takes that slot off. You go back to the grey starter look for that part.
  • Monsters are their own tab. Tapping one transforms you; "Back to my outfit" (or tapping any clothing) switches it off. Your outfit is kept underneath.
  • Items you just brewed say NEW until you've looked at them; a dot on the tab shows where they are.
  • Rarer items are sorted first, with the rarity color on the border.
  • Only items you own show up. Nothing you haven't brewed yet appears, not even as a blank tile.
  • "Brew" at the bottom goes to the Cauldron. With no tokens it says how to earn one.
